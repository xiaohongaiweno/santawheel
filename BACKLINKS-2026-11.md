## 二、可直接粘贴的英文素材

### 一句话定位（60 字符内）

```
Draw Secret Santa names in one click — no sign-up, no emails required.
```

### 短描述（Product Hunt / 目录通用，260 字符内）

```
SantaWheel is a free Secret Santa generator. Paste your list, set exclusions for couples or managers, and draw names in one click. Send assignments by email or by private links — no participant email addresses needed. Organizer blind mode, self-join links, scheduled draws, and anonymous questions included.
```

### 长描述（AlternativeTo / SaaSHub）

```
SantaWheel draws Secret Santa and gift-exchange names for free, with no account required.

How it works: paste a list of names (commas or one per line), optionally add exclusion pairs so partners, roommates, or a manager and their direct report never draw each other, then draw. Every participant gets exactly one person and nobody draws themselves. If your rules make a valid draw impossible, the site says so instead of returning a broken pairing.

Two ways to deliver assignments:
- Email — the site sends each participant their person automatically.
- Private links — no participant emails collected at all. You get one link per person to forward in WhatsApp, Messenger, or a group chat.

Also included: organizer blind mode (the dashboard never shows pairings, not even to the organizer), self-join links so people add their own names, scheduled draws that run automatically at a set time, anonymous questions to your giftee, and shipping-address collection for gifts that get mailed. Up to 100 participants per draw.

Free for the core flow. An optional $4.99 organizer upgrade adds CSV/Excel import, automatic reminders for people who haven't opened their assignment, and no-repeat draws across years.

There are also rules guides for white elephant, yankee swap, and dirty santa if your group plays one of those instead:
https://santawheel.com/white-elephant-rules
https://santawheel.com/yankee-swap-rules
https://santawheel.com/dirty-santa-rules
```

### 分类 / 标签

- 类别：Lifestyle、Productivity、Web App、Fun
- 标签：secret santa, gift exchange, name picker, random name generator, christmas, white elephant, yankee swap, office party

I built SantaWheel because every December our office draw ended in the same mess: someone drew their partner, someone's email bounced, and the organizer ended up forwarding screenshots.

What it does differently:

- Private links mode, so you never collect anyone's email
- Blind mode, where the organizer genuinely cannot see the pairings
- Scheduled draws — set Friday 6pm, close the laptop

It's free and there's no sign-up. Happy to answer anything about how the draw handles impossible exclusion sets.
